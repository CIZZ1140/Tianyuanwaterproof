## SEO and SEM reasoning

- The title and first screen now match a precise material query (`laminated asphalt shingles`) and clarify architectural roof supply. This may improve relevance for product searches and reduce clicks from people looking for generic shingle advice. A more specific title can attract less broad, low-intent traffic.
- The meta description presents piece count, size and coverage. These terms give a buyer a reason to click and can align with specification-oriented searches; Google may rewrite the snippet, so its appearance is not guaranteed.
- A single unchanged H1 remains consistent with the URL and product name. Subsections explain takeoff, samples, system quotation and RFQ needs rather than repeating the 3-tab page's generic benefits. This improves landing-page usefulness for procurement-oriented ads.
- The quote and document actions preserve tracked product/source parameters. The page has no public fixed price or review rating, which fits a configuration-dependent B2B quote.
- The two related shingles offer a close comparison path. The existing gallery's placeholder was removed so image-search and ad visitors see a real product image rather than an empty asset.
- Trade-off: removing dramatic wind/fire claims gives up those unqualified keyword phrases and sales hooks. Such classifications should only be restored after grade-specific reports and the tested assembly are available. Campaign copy must use the same qualification as the landing page.

The connected GSC property's exact-page Performance report showed **0 clicks and 0 impressions for 2026-07-17 through 2026-10-16** (checked 2026-10-19). It provided no search queries to prioritize, so keyword intent was derived from the product and market evidence above. No Ads quality-score or conversion lift is claimed. Rankings, CTR and leads require post-deployment observation over a meaningful period.

## Evidence consulted

Three user catalogues were inspected. `Tianyuan Catalog 2026.pdf` and `tianyuan catalog.pdf` had no usable laminated-shingle table. `E-catalogues.pdf`, PDF page 9, contained an older laminated/plane shingle table: laminated soluble content 1800 g/m² and shared shingle indices for tensile 500/400 N/50 mm, 90°C heat resistance, 10°C flexibility and ≥75 N nail pull. Its visible DONGSHENG branding means it is supporting historical supplier material, **not** a current Tianyuan TDS or certification.

Domestic producer sites and catalogues: [Hebei Sinostar](https://www.hbsinostar.com/roofing-sheet/asphalt-shingles/architectural-shingles.html), [SKW Roof](https://skwroof.com/asphalt-shingles/laminated-shingles/), [Cailin catalogue](https://fr.cailinroofing.com/uploads/Cailin-Asphalt-Shingle-Catalog4.pdf), and [Sangobuild catalogue](https://sangobuildroofing.com/files/SANGOBUILD%20ASPHALT%20SHINGLE%20CATALOG-202205280639488456.pdf).
