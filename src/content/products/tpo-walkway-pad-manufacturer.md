## The Most Walked-On Part of a Roof Fails First

On a TPO single-ply roof, the waterproofing membrane is engineered for decades of UV, weather and thermal cycling. What it is not engineered for is being walked on. Every service visit — HVAC check, solar panel wash, roof survey, drain clearing — concentrates foot traffic, ladders and tools onto the same narrow routes, and over time that traffic abrades, scores and punctures the membrane in exactly the zones where repairs are hardest to make.

The TY-SHIELD™ TPO Walkway Pad is the answer to that specific problem: a dedicated walking surface that takes the traffic instead of the membrane.

### Not a Mat — an Extension of the Roof System

The distinction matters. A rubber mat is an independent layer: it sits on the membrane, ages at its own rate, moves with temperature at its own rate, and relies on adhesion that degrades in UV. The TPO walkway pad is manufactured from a TPO formulation in the same polymer family as TPO roofing membranes — which is what makes hot-air welding possible. The pad welds into the membrane the same way two membrane sheets weld together: one continuous, watertight surface, with no adhesive line and no fasteners through the waterproofing layer.

### Where the Walkway Earns Its Place

The pad is specified for the zones where a roof actually gets used: the route from the access door to the HVAC units, the service path along solar arrays, the working area around drains and roof penetrations, and the staging zone where equipment gets hauled up. On occupied buildings — offices, retail, logistics — a defined walking line is not just membrane protection, it is a safety feature that keeps maintenance crews on a slip-resistant, clearly marked path.

For the membrane itself, the pad works as a load-distributing shield. Foot traffic and equipment concentrate force onto small contact points; the pad spreads that load and absorbs the abrasion, extending the membrane's service performance in the areas that would otherwise wear out first.

### Built for the Roof, Not Just for the Warehouse

The pad arrives in light, manageable rolls — cut to length on site, positioned along the planned route, welded in with the same hot-air equipment already on the roof for the membrane. No kettles, no heavy panels, no special crews. It is a product that understands the rhythm of a roofing job: plan the route, roll it out, weld it in, move on.

For projects where the full system matters, pair the walkway with our [TPO Waterproofing Membrane](/products/tpo-waterproofing-membrane-manufacturer/) for a complete exposed roof assembly — and where PVC or EPDM systems are specified, the same walkway principle applies with the membrane-matched protection product.

### The Specification Question

The most common question from buyers is "what thickness?" — and the honest answer is that it depends on traffic. 1.5mm covers light inspection routes; 2.0mm is the balanced default for regular HVAC service; 2.5mm–3.0mm is for equipment-heavy zones where carts, ladders and tools live permanently. Width follows the walking line: 500mm suits single-file access routes, 600mm gives crews a more comfortable working width, and OEM widths up to 1200mm cover open service decks.
