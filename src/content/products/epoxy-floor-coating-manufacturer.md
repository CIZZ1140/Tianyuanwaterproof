### Specify the floor system before comparing material prices

An epoxy color-sand floor is purchased as a layered assembly. Colored quartz and epoxy binder create the decorative layer, but the concrete condition, primer, repair mortar and finish coat determine whether the installed floor will meet its intended traffic and cleaning demands. A bid that omits those layers may look cheaper while describing a different floor.

For a useful B2B comparison, define the substrate profile, moisture condition, total thickness, traffic, chemical exposure and the required surface texture. Smooth and textured finishes should not share an assumed slip value. The initial sample should represent both the approved color blend and the actual sealing method.

The technical indices above provide a procurement starting point for flow, drying, hardness, abrasion, adhesion, slip, water resistance and installed-system strength. Set the final acceptance criteria against the offered kit and the project design, including the required test method and conditioning period. An A+B mix ratio, VOC result or opening-to-traffic time must come from that kit's current documents rather than a generic epoxy listing.
