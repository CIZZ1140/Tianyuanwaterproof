## High-Performance Thermoplastic Polyolefin Roofing Membrane

TPO (Thermoplastic Polyolefin) waterproofing membrane is a single-ply polymer sheet that has become the fastest-growing segment in the global commercial roofing market. It combines the flexibility of rubber with the heat-weldability of thermoplastics.
