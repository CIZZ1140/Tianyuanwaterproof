faqs:
  - question: "What is the difference between Class N and Class PY self-adhesive membranes?"
    answer: "Class N (Non-Reinforcement) membranes have no internal carrier and rely on the polymer-modified bitumen compound itself for mechanical integrity. They are available in thinner gauges (1.2–2.0mm) and are suitable for less demanding applications. Class PY (Polyester Felt) membranes incorporate a high-strength polyester reinforcement carrier, offering thicknesses of 2.0–4.0mm with significantly higher tensile strength and puncture resistance — the preferred choice for heavy-duty roofing and below-grade waterproofing. We also offer glass fiber felt (G) reinforced variants for specialized applications."
  - question: "Which surface material should I choose — PE, PET, aluminum foil, or sand?"
    answer: "PE film is the most economical and widely used — suitable for general roofing and underground applications where UV exposure is not a concern. PET film offers higher reflectivity and heat resistance. Aluminum foil provides superior UV reflection, vapor barrier properties, and aesthetic finish for exposed applications. Sand/mineral (D) surface is designed for torch-on system compatibility and slip resistance. Our sales team can recommend the optimal surface for your specific project conditions."
  - question: "What is the difference between single-side and double-side self-adhesive membrane?"
    answer: "Single-side self-adhesive has adhesive coating on the bottom face only, with a surface film (PE/PET/ALU/Sand) on top — it is the standard for most roofing and basement applications where the membrane is the outermost waterproofing layer. Double-side self-adhesive has release liners on both faces, allowing it to bond two substrates together — commonly used as a sandwich layer between concrete slabs, for lap-joint reinforcement, or where additional bonding strength is required."
  - question: "Is a primer required before installing self-adhesive membrane?"
    answer: "While the membrane can bond directly to clean, smooth metal and some non-porous surfaces, we strongly recommend applying a cold bitumen primer on concrete, masonry, and blockwork. Primer seals the substrate surface, eliminates dust, and significantly enhances long-term peel adhesion — especially critical in below-grade applications where hydrostatic pressure is a concern."
  - question: "Can self-adhesive membrane be installed in cold weather?"
    answer: "Installation is not recommended below 5°C as the adhesive compound becomes less tacky. However, gentle indirect warming of the adhesive face (using a hot air gun on low setting, not an open flame) before application can restore bond performance. For projects requiring winter installation, please contact us about our enhanced cold-weather formulation options."
  - question: "How does self-adhesive membrane compare to torch-applied SBS membrane?"
    answer: "Self-adhesive membrane is the safer, faster alternative — no open flame, no hot-work permit, no specialized torching equipment. Torch-applied SBS membranes offer higher heat resistance and are preferred for exposed roofing in hot climates. Self-adhesive membranes excel in confined spaces, refurbishment projects, chemical plants, and anywhere fire safety is paramount. Many projects use both: torch-on SBS for exposed roofs and self-adhesive for basement and foundation systems."
  - question: "What is the minimum overlap for self-adhesive membrane seams?"
    answer: "Side laps require a minimum 100mm overlap; end laps require a minimum 150mm overlap. All seams must be firmly rolled with a hand roller to ensure a continuous, monolithic waterproof seal. Stagger end laps between adjacent rows by at least 300mm to avoid creating a weak point."
  - question: "What is the shelf life and how should rolls be stored?"
    answer: "Shelf life is 12 to 18 months from the production date when stored properly: vertically in original packaging, in a cool, dry, covered area away from direct sunlight, at temperatures between +5°C and +35°C. Do not stack rolls horizontally during long-term storage as this can cause deformation. Remove packaging only at the moment of application."
