## 3-Tab Roofing for Repeatable Project Takeoffs

This single-layer asphalt shingle uses a fiberglass mat within the asphalt coating and a colored mineral-granule face. Three evenly cut tabs create a consistent course pattern. It is intended for exposed pitched roofs when combined with the correct deck, underlayment, starter, fixing and flashing details.

For procurement, first compare the 1000 × 333 mm strip, nominal 2.6 mm thickness and 21-strip bundle against the roof design. At an exposure of approximately 148 mm, the reference bundle covers about 3.1 m²; ridge, valley and cutting waste require a separate allowance. The proposed supply should be matched to the destination's wind and fire documentation requirements rather than treating an individual shingle as a complete rated roof assembly.
