## Dimensional roofing for project supply

Laminated asphalt shingles use bonded layers to create a varied shadow line on pitched roofs. The proposed 1000 × 333 mm, 16-piece bundle format gives purchasers a starting point for comparing appearance, installed coverage and transport weight. Final color, dimensions and technical performance should be agreed against the offered sample and current documents.

For a project quote, send the roof plan, pitch, destination market and required roof-system details. We can align shingle quantity with starter, underlayment, flashing and ridge materials instead of quoting the field shingles in isolation.
