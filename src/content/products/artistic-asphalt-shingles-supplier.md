## Shaped Shingles for Project Roof Design

Fish-scale and mosaic asphalt shingles offer two distinct cut patterns within a fiberglass-reinforced, mineral-granule roof covering. The rounded fish-scale edge gives a softer repeated course, while the mosaic cut creates an angular, hexagonal-looking field. Both are intended for pitched-roof assemblies designed with the correct deck, underlayment, fixing and edge details.

For procurement, identify the profile and approve the actual color sample first. The supply format is 1000 ± 3 × 333 ± 3 mm, 2.6 ± 0.1 mm thick and 21 strips per bundle for either cut. The laying exposure and roof shape determine installed coverage; send a roof plan so the quotation can include starter, ridge and detail quantities alongside the field shingles.
