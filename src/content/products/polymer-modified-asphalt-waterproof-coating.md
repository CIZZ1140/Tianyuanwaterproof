### Select the grade, then design the layer

Polymer modified asphalt coating is a water-based option for buyers who need a cold-applied bituminous waterproofing layer on prepared concrete and difficult details. It forms a continuous film after drying, making it useful where a roll membrane would need many cuts and overlaps. The coating is part of a designed waterproofing assembly, with the finished layer protected where required.

The grade decision has a practical trade-off. L grade combines 80 ± 2°C heat resistance with −15°C low-temperature flexibility; H grade increases the heat-resistance figure to 110 ± 2°C while its standard low-temperature figure is 0°C. Both grades share the listed solid-content, bond-strength, watertightness and elongation indices. A buyer should select the grade against the actual climate and assembly rather than choosing H solely because its heat number is higher.

For a useful project inquiry, send the grade, substrate, total area, design thickness, protection layer and destination port. We can then align the quotation and technical documents with the requested supply configuration.
