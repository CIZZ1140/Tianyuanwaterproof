## Selecting the Right PU Coating for the Project

Single-component polyurethane coating is used where a liquid-applied waterproofing layer must follow the shape of the substrate and its details. Unlike a sheet membrane, it does not require a cut lap at every change of direction. The continuity of the finished layer still depends on sound substrate preparation, correct treatment of joints and penetrations, and the specified film build.

Tianyuan offers Type I and Type II grades. Type I provides greater elongation for assemblies where movement is a priority; Type II provides higher tensile and tear strength. Share the intended use, substrate and project requirements when selecting a grade.

### Installation Details That Need a System Decision

Substrate condition determines preparation and primer selection. Corners, drains, cracks and other movement points may need reinforcement. Specify a protective finish for assemblies where the coating will not remain exposed.
