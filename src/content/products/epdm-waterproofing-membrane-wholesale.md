faqs:
  - question: "What is the difference between vulcanized and non-vulcanized EPDM?"
    answer: "Vulcanized (JL1) EPDM undergoes a curing process that cross-links the polymer chains, delivering superior tensile strength (≥7.5 MPa), higher tear resistance (≥25 kN/m), and better low-temperature flexibility (-40°C). Non-vulcanized (JL1) EPDM is easier to bond and offers excellent hot-air aging retention (≥90% tensile), with slightly lower mechanical properties (≥4.0 MPa tensile). Vulcanized is the recommended choice for exposed roofing and heavy-duty applications; non-vulcanized is preferred where ease of installation and bonding are priorities."

## The Gold Standard in Synthetic Polymer Waterproofing

EPDM (Ethylene-Propylene Diene Monomer) rubber waterproofing membrane is the longest-serving synthetic polymer waterproofing material in the construction industry, with documented installations exceeding 50 years of continuous service. Its secret is chemistry: EPDM's saturated molecular backbone contains no reactive double bonds, giving it unmatched resistance to UV radiation, ozone, thermal oxidation, and environmental aging — properties that no other polymer waterproofing membrane can match over comparable timescales.

TY-SHIELD™ EPDM membranes are manufactured from ethylene-propylene rubber compounded with nano materials, anti-aging agents, and curing promoters. The resulting sheet combines extraordinary elongation (≥450%), high tensile strength, and flexibility at temperatures from -40°C to +160°C. Available in both vulcanized (JL1) and non-vulcanized (JL1) types, in homogeneous (H) and fabric-backed (L) variants, our EPDM serves the most demanding waterproofing applications — from luxury residential roofing to heavy civil infrastructure.

All products comply with **GB/T 18173.1-2012**. As a direct manufacturer, Weifang Tianyuan supplies international distributors, engineering contractors, and civil project procurement teams with consistent, batch-tested quality and complete OEM flexibility.

### Engineered for the Most Demanding Environments

EPDM's performance advantages are not marketing claims — they are the direct result of its molecular architecture. The saturated polymer backbone means there are no vulnerable double bonds for UV, ozone, or oxygen to attack. This is the same chemistry that makes EPDM the material of choice for automotive weather seals, electrical cable insulation, and industrial hoses exposed to the elements for decades.

In waterproofing, this translates to:

1. **Anti-aging** — service life up to 50 years, the longest among all polymer waterproofing membranes
2. **High elongation** — ≥450% at break, absorbing structural movement and thermal expansion without cracking
3. **Plant root resistance** — suitable for green roofs and planting roofs without additional root barriers
4. **Modified bonding** — specially engineered molecular structure overcomes traditional EPDM adhesion challenges
5. **Low-temperature flexibility** — maintains pliability at -40°C (vulcanized), adapting to extreme climate swings
6. **Convenient installation** — adhesive-bonded system, no hot work, no specialized welding equipment
7. **Chemical corrosion resistance** — suitable for chemically aggressive environments and special industrial applications
8. **Easy maintenance** — repairable with compatible adhesive and patch material, low lifecycle cost
9. **Detail-friendly** — flexible and conformable around penetrations, corners, and complex architectural details
10. **Puncture resistance** — high mechanical strength protects the membrane during and after construction
