### Cement-Bonded Composite Waterproofing for Every Surface

PP/PE (Polyethylene Polypropylene) composite waterproof membrane is a multi-layer high-polymer waterproofing system that bonds directly to substrates using cement-based adhesive — no torches, no solvents, no specialized equipment. This fundamental difference from bitumen and single-ply polymer membranes makes it the product of choice for indoor wet areas, damp-substrate applications, and any project where tiles or screeds are the final surface finish.

TY-SHIELD™ PP/PE membrane is built with a precisely engineered 5-layer structure: PP non-woven fabric on both surfaces provides the mechanical bonding interface — a porous, fibrous matrix that polymer cement mortar penetrates and locks into. Inside, a dense PE (polyethylene) core film delivers the primary waterproofing barrier. EVA-based auxiliary adhesive layers bond the PP fleece to the PE core, creating a unified composite sheet that combines waterproof integrity with full-surface adhesion.

### Five Engineering Advantages of PP/PE Composite

1. **Polymer Fiber-Reinforced Adhesion** — The PP non-woven fiber surface forms a full mechanical bond with polymer cement waterproof adhesive, creating a monolithic waterproofing system where the membrane, adhesive, and substrate work as one.
2. **Cavity-Filling & Channel-Sealing** — During application, the cement-based adhesive fills small holes, cracks, and rough areas in the substrate, preventing water from channeling beneath the membrane — a risk in mechanical-fastened or self-adhesive systems.
3. **Full Adhesion on Damp Substrates** — The cement hydration process allows the adhesive to cure and bond even on damp concrete, eliminating drying delays and enabling fast-track construction schedules.
4. **Direct Finish Compatibility** — The non-woven fiber surface accepts tiles, cement screeds, and plaster directly — no separate bonding primers or mechanical fixing layers required between the waterproofing and the finished surface.
5. **Root Penetration Resistance** — High puncture resistance and dense composite structure provide effective barrier performance against plant roots, making the membrane suitable for green roofs and landscaped podium decks.
