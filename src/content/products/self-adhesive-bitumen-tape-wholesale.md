### Aluminum-Faced Bitumen Flashing for Defined Roof Details

This self-adhesive bitumen flashing tape is supplied as a faced strip, rather than a full-width waterproofing membrane or an unfaced joint filler. Its modified-bitumen adhesive is protected by a removable liner until installation; the aluminum surface remains visible in the selected roof or gutter detail.

For procurement, compare the complete construction of each roll: facing, adhesive thickness, width, length and substrate. The technical indices above can be specified for a project, while the offered grade and its test record should match the final order. The −20°C bend check and heat test describe laboratory performance, not a blanket site-application temperature or service-life promise.

When the work involves a large roof area, specify the primary waterproofing membrane and use flashing tape where the system detail calls for it. For coated metal, old bitumen and porous mineral substrates, approve a representative sample before placing the bulk order.
