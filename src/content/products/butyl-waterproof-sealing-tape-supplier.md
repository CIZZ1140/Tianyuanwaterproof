### Specify the tape by joint, facing and substrate

A butyl seal is a joint detail rather than a universal repair strip. The same roll width can perform differently on coated steel, aluminum, cementitious board and polymer membrane. For a comparable project quotation, provide the joint drawing, substrate finish, expected movement, water exposure and the required protective facing.

The technical targets above are a starting point for a single-sided tape used on metal-sheet joints. Agree the exact product classification, test substrate and acceptance record for the supplied grade. Laboratory heat and cold-bend checks do not establish the installation temperature or an unconditional outdoor service life.

Pressure-sensitive butyl needs continuous contact. A clean, sound surface, a compatible primer where needed, careful rolling and suitable compression or cover detailing matter as much as the nominal peel figure. Order a representative roll sample when the project uses coated metal, porous concrete or a membrane with uncertain compatibility.
