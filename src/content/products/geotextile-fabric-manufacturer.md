## Nonwoven Geotextile for Project Procurement

A needle-punched nonwoven can act as a separator, a filter or a protective cushion, but the correct grade depends on the site assembly. This page focuses on **staple-fiber PP or PET rolls**. If your specification calls for a continuous-filament construction, request a separate offer and test schedule.

## Select the Performance That Matters

For a drainage filter, provide the soil gradation and required hydraulic performance; opening size and water flow matter alongside mass. For road separation, provide the subgrade and aggregate loading conditions. For geomembrane cushioning, identify the liner, support surface and puncture exposure. A roll described only as “300 gsm geotextile” does not establish suitability for all three uses.

## Build a Complete RFQ

Send the required polymer, nominal mass or strength class, tensile and puncture targets, opening-size and permeability requirements, roll dimensions, project quantity and destination. State the governing standard and test methods. The values above are **reference indices for quotation**; the offered grade's technical data and any required test documents establish the final specification.
