### A Tacky Interface for Protected Waterproofing Systems

Non-curing rubber asphalt coating occupies a different role from a coating that dries into an exposed film. It is heated for application, then remains a tacky paste beneath the next system layer. For a buyer, the key decision is whether the coating, sheet membrane and protection build-up are specified as one assembly.

The technical table on this page covers solids, flash point, extensibility, adhesion failure mode, temperature response and chemical-resistance checks. Thickness, material consumption and installation temperature must be selected for the actual substrate and membrane pairing; a drum weight alone cannot determine the area covered.

### Specify the Coating and Sheet Together

For basement slabs, protected roofs and podiums, identify the sheet facing, lap method, movement details and protective layer before ordering. A continuous coating-to-sheet interface can help reduce paths for water migration when it is installed and detailed correctly. It does not make penetrations, joints or damaged sheets repair-free.
