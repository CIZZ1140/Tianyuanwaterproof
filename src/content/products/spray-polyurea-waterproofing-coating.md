### Compare the full spray system, not only the coating price

Spray polyurea is a reactive A+B membrane installed with heated plural-component equipment. Pure polyurea and polyurethane–polyurea hybrid have different performance indices and may require different operating settings. A procurement comparison should therefore name the grade, intended assembly and acceptance criteria before comparing unit prices.

The pure grade is listed at 100% solid content, ≥20 MPa tensile strength and ≥600% elongation. The hybrid is listed at ≥98% solids, ≥22 MPa tensile strength and ≥500% elongation. These are separate product profiles rather than one combined claim. Grade selection also depends on substrate preparation, primer, detailed junctions, film thickness and the finish or protection above the membrane.

For a firm quotation, state the coated area, substrate, design dry-film thickness, exposure, available spray equipment and destination port. Ask for matched A+B quantities and the current grade-specific technical documents. A rapid gel time alone does not establish when a bridge deck, floor or tank can return to service.
