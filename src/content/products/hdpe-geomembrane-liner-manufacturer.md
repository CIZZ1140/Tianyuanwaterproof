## HDPE Geomembrane for Engineered Containment

This roll liner is intended for welded containment assemblies. Start the RFQ with the **application, thickness and surface finish**, then specify mechanical and durability indices. The 1.5 mm smooth-sheet figures above give buyers a comparable starting schedule; they are not a substitute for the offered grade's current technical data and project quality records.

## Select Smooth and Textured Grades Separately

Smooth sheet is often used across prepared base areas. On slopes, a textured interface may be needed to meet a project-specific shear requirement. Texture selection should follow the actual soil, geotextile and cover interfaces; smooth-sheet tensile and puncture figures should not be copied into a textured-grade schedule.

## Quote the Installed System

Roll width can reduce the number of field seams, but handling access, panel layout and container limits also matter. Send the site drawings and quantity together with required seam testing, protective geotextile, welding accessories and roll documentation. This produces a quotation the contractor can relate to the installed barrier, not just the sheet area.
