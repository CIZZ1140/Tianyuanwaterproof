### Select the grade for the substrate and movement

JS polymer cement waterproof coating combines a liquid polymer component with a cement-based powder. The three listed grades have different performance profiles: Type I emphasizes elongation, Type II balances elongation and strength, and Type III has the highest listed adhesion strength. The comparison above helps a procurement team choose a starting grade before requesting a project quotation.

The coating is intended for prepared mineral substrates in a designed waterproofing assembly. Bathrooms, kitchens, below-grade walls and protected balcony or roof areas place different demands on movement accommodation, water exposure and the final protective layer. Share those conditions with the required quantity and destination so the offered grade, packing and installation documents match the job.

For a project that calls for a more elastic single-component liquid system, compare the [polyurethane waterproof coating](/products/polyurethane-waterproof-coating-factory/).
