## Quick Answer: What Determines the Price of SBS Waterproofing Membrane?

The price of SBS membrane is determined by **specification — thickness, reinforcement, surface finish, performance grade, and container loading efficiency — not by the material type alone**. Bitumen feedstock costs (driven by crude oil) set the market baseline, but the specification you choose moves the price more than any single external factor. Compare quotations only on identical specifications, and always request TDS, MSDS and batch test reports to verify what you are actually paying for.

## 1. Six Specification Factors That Move the Price

B2B buyers often assume the brand or the country of origin drives the price. In practice, six specification decisions move the number far more:

| Factor | Options | How It Affects Price |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Thickness** | 3mm / 4mm / 5mm | Thicker membrane = more bitumen and reinforcement per m². A 5mm roll costs significantly more per m² than 3mm, and fits less per container |
| **Reinforcement** | Polyester (PY) / Fiberglass (G) / Composite (PYG) | Polyester and composite carriers cost more than fiberglass but deliver higher tensile strength and elongation for demanding substrates |
| **Surface Finish** | PE film / Fine sand / Mineral granules / Aluminum foil | Mineral granules (slate chips) and foil add raw material and processing cost; PE film is the economical standard for covered systems |
| **Performance Grade** | Type I / Type II | Type II adds low-temperature flexibility (-25°C vs -20°C), heat resistance (105°C vs 90°C) and higher tensile values — the premium is justified where the project demands it |
| **Roll Dimensions** | 1m × 10m standard, customizable | Custom roll lengths change packaging cost and installation waste |
| **Container Loading** | 3mm ≈ 4,000-5,000 m² / 4mm ≈ 3,000-4,000 m² / 5mm ≈ 2,500-3,500 m² per 20FT | Loading efficiency directly affects freight cost per m² — one of the largest hidden cost differences between quotations |

**The crude oil baseline.** Bitumen is a byproduct of oil refining, so raw bitumen is the largest single cost component of any modified bitumen membrane, and crude oil price swings periodically move every supplier's quotations. This baseline affects all suppliers equally — which is exactly why you should compare specifications, not try to time the oil market.

## 2. How to Compare Quotations Fairly

A lower price on a different specification is not a lower price. To compare quotations meaningfully:

1. **Fix the specification first.** Send every supplier the same thickness, reinforcement, surface and grade — ideally referencing the standard (e.g., GB/T 18242, Type II, PY, 4mm).
2. **Align the trade terms.** Compare like for like: FOB vs CIF, the same MOQ, the same payment terms (e.g., T/T 30% advance, 70% against B/L copy), and the same delivery lead time.
3. **Verify the TDS matches the quote.** A quoted "4mm polyester" that is actually a lighter reinforcement is a different product at a different price.
4. **Factor in loading.** Ask for the container loading plan (m² per 20FT/40FT) — it converts the unit price into the true landed cost per m².
5. **Request samples.** Sample availability and courier terms vary by supplier and product; request samples or batch test reports when they are relevant to the specification.

## 3. Documents Every Serious Supplier Should Provide

Before you commit to a quotation, the supplier should be able to provide:

| Document | What It Proves |
| :--- | :--- |
| **TDS (Technical Data Sheet)** | The performance values: soluble content, tensile strength, elongation, low-temperature flexibility, heat resistance, watertightness |
| **MSDS / SDS** | Handling, storage and transport safety data |
| **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** | Batch-specific test results for the actual shipment |
| **Batch test report** | Third-party or factory-laboratory evidence matching the TDS values |
| **Packing list** | Roll count, m², gross/net weight, pallet configuration |

For export projects, confirm the test standard relevant to your region — GB/T 18242, ASTM, or EN — and ask for evidence against that standard, not a generic compliance claim.

China remains the global hub for waterproofing production, but the quality gap between suppliers is widening. When evaluating a manufacturer like [Tianyuan Waterproof](/about/), use these three checks:

1. **Technical Density (the gram weight).** Do not just ask for "polyester." Ask for the reinforcement gram weight per square meter (g/m²). A 180g/m² reinforcement offers significantly better puncture resistance than a lighter alternative — and the difference shows up in the batch test report.
2. **Verification of flexibility.** Request the -20°C / -25°C bend test result from the batch report, or a video of the test. High-quality SBS membranes show no cracking when bent around a 25mm mandrel at sub-zero temperatures.
3. **Audit the export compliance.** Confirm the factory can provide documentation for your region — ASTM for the Americas, EN for Europe, or GB/T 18242 for international engineering.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do SBS membrane prices vary between suppliers?

The price difference is driven by specification — thickness (3mm, 4mm or 5mm), reinforcement (polyester, fiberglass or composite), surface finish (PE film, sand, granules or foil), performance grade (Type I or II) and container loading efficiency — not by the supplier's brand. Bitumen feedstock costs, linked to crude oil, set the market baseline, but two quotations only become comparable when they cover identical specifications and trade terms.

### What documents should I request from an SBS membrane supplier?

Request the TDS (technical data sheet), MSDS/SDS, certificate of analysis, batch test report and packing list before ordering. For export projects, confirm the membrane is tested to the standard relevant to your region — GB/T 18242, ASTM or EN — and ask for the corresponding test evidence rather than a generic claim.

### How should I compare quotations from different SBS suppliers?

Ask every supplier to quote on the same specification and trade terms: identical thickness, reinforcement, surface finish and grade, the same FOB or CIF basis, the same MOQ and payment terms, and the same delivery lead time. Verify that the quoted specification matches the TDS before comparing unit prices. A lower price on a different specification is not a lower price.

### What is the typical MOQ and container loading for SBS membrane?

The typical MOQ is one 20FT container per specification, with mixed-container loading available. As a reference, a 20FT container holds approximately 4,000-5,000 m² of 3mm membrane, 3,000-4,000 m² of 4mm, and 2,500-3,500 m² of 5mm. Actual loading depends on roll weight, reinforcement, surface finish and destination-port weight limits — request a loading plan with your quotation.

### How can I verify SBS membrane quality before ordering?

Ask the supplier for the batch test report covering soluble content, tensile strength, elongation, low-temperature flexibility and watertightness, and compare it against the TDS. Request free A4 samples for physical evaluation, and arrange third-party inspection before shipment if the order size justifies it.
