## Start With the Tunnel Design, Then Choose the Membrane

**Choose a tunnel waterproofing membrane by construction method, groundwater design and access for installation.** A welded PVC sheet between tunnel linings, a pre-applied HDPE membrane against future concrete and a bonded SBS membrane on an accessible cut-and-cover structure perform different roles. A material name or thickness alone does not define a waterproof tunnel.

This guide helps contractors and procurement teams prepare a system comparison. For the application overview, visit [Tunnel Waterproofing Solutions](/applications/tunnel-waterproofing/). For accessible basement walls and foundation slabs, use the separate [SBS below-grade waterproofing guide](/blog/sbs-membrane-basement-foundation-waterproofing/).

## Compare PVC, Pre-Applied HDPE and SBS by Construction Sequence

| Project condition | System to evaluate | What must be resolved before approval |
|---|---|---|
| Excavated tunnel with a primary support and a later concrete lining | Tunnel-grade welded PVC sheet with the specified cushion or drainage arrangement | Fixing method, substrate profile, welding access, joint waterstops and membrane protection during lining works |
| Blind-side cut-and-cover wall or slab, with concrete cast against the waterproofing | A pre-applied membrane designed to bond to fresh concrete | Approved orientation, laps, connections to adjacent systems, exposure before casting and repair of construction damage |
| Accessible external face of a cut-and-cover box | Bonded SBS, self-adhesive bitumen or another approved post-applied system | Substrate condition, bonding method, continuity around corners and protection before backfill |
| Project with restrictions on open-flame work | An approved flame-free sheet or bonding system | Adhesive or welding equipment requirements, ventilation and site safety controls; flame-free does not remove every installation hazard |

The comparison is a shortlist, not an interchangeability statement. A roofing PVC sheet is not automatically a tunnel-grade product. A smooth HDPE geomembrane liner is not the same product as a pre-applied concrete-bonding membrane. Check the intended use and complete system documentation.

## Decide Whether the Tunnel Is Drained or Undrained

Ask the designer to identify the groundwater strategy before requesting membrane prices. In a drained arrangement, water is directed to designed collection and drainage paths. In an undrained arrangement, the structure and waterproofing design must account for the specified water pressure. Maintenance access to drains, groundwater restrictions and the continuity of the invert detail belong in this decision.

**Do not convert a laboratory watertightness test into a permissible tunnel depth.** The specified design head, seams, penetrations, support and protection all affect the installed system. There is no universal pressure threshold that selects PVC over HDPE or SBS for every project.

The [ITA-AITES overview of tunnel equipment and waterproofing](https://tunnel.ita-aites.org/en/how-to-go-underground/operation/equipment) explains the distinction between drainage-based and watertight arrangements. Use the project's drawings to establish which arrangement is being procured.

## Layer Build-Up and Interfaces to Confirm

For a sheet system between primary support and a secondary lining, a discussion drawing may show the primary support, cushion or drainage layer, waterproofing membrane and final concrete lining. That sequence is illustrative: cushion weight, membrane thickness, drainage position and fixing details must come from the approved design.

Review the following interfaces together:

- **Substrate to membrane:** sharp projections, fixing points and local hollows need treatment compatible with the selected system.
- **Sheet to sheet:** confirm the membrane formulation and the permitted welding or bonding method. Keep compatible repair material available.
- **Membrane to joint system:** show construction joints, movement joints, waterstops and any compartment or injection details on coordinated drawings.
- **Membrane to concrete works:** define protection at reinforcement supports, penetrations, formwork and the next concrete pour.
- **Tunnel to adjacent structure:** portals, shafts and transitions to cut-and-cover sections need a continuous detail even when the membrane family changes.

A geotextile cushion does not by itself establish a drainage design. A membrane reinforcement carrier also does not replace an engineered movement-joint assembly.

## Seam Inspection and Hold Points Before Covering

The installation method statement should identify the seam type and its inspection method. Where a qualified double-track welded seam is used, its test channel can support air-pressure testing under the approved procedure. Other seams, patches and terminations require their own accepted checks. Do not prescribe one test or welding technique for every material and detail.

| Hold point | Evidence to retain | Reason to stop covering the work |
|---|---|---|
| Before membrane placement | Substrate acceptance, approved materials and installation conditions | Sharp projections, water ingress or an unapproved substrate condition |
| At the start of welding or after conditions change | Trial seam and agreed test record | Trial seam does not meet the project acceptance criteria |
| After each accessible membrane area is completed | Seam map, inspection results, repair locations and photographs | An untested seam, unresolved defect or inaccessible repair |
| Before reinforcement, lining concrete or backfill | Signed inspection release and protection check | Damage from following trades or incomplete transitions |

Specify who inspects and accepts each stage. Keep the inspection records traceable to the tunnel section rather than only to a shipment of membrane rolls.

## When SBS Bitumen Is a Candidate

SBS can be considered for selected protected cut-and-cover structures where the substrate, access and application method suit the approved design. It should not be substituted for an inter-lining PVC system simply because both products are waterproof sheets. ## Compare Installed Scope, Not Unsupported Lifespan Promises

A useful tender comparison includes preparation, cushion or drainage layers, membrane, joints, protection, testing and repairs before covering. Compare these items on the same area and drawing revision. Avoid generic service-life promises or maintenance-cost figures without project-specific evidence. Inaccessible repair after lining can matter more than a small difference in roll price.

## Tunnel Waterproofing Procurement Checklist

Send the following with a [technical document and quotation request](/contact/):

1. Tunnel construction method, cross-section and installation sequence.
2. Drained or undrained design, groundwater conditions and specified design head.
3. Membrane location, available access and the approved layer build-up.
4. Required material, thickness and product-specific test criteria.
5. Joint, penetration, shaft and portal transition details.
6. Seam inspection procedure and acceptance responsibilities.
7. Quantity, roll dimensions, packing and destination requirements.

## FAQ

### What is the best waterproofing membrane for tunnels?

The best membrane depends on the tunnel type, groundwater condition, installation method and structural design. PVC and HDPE membranes are commonly used for sprayed concrete-lined tunnels, while SBS bitumen membranes are used in selected cut-and-cover and protected underground systems. No single membrane is suitable for all tunnel projects.

### Can bitumen membranes be used in tunnel waterproofing?

SBS modified bitumen membranes can be used in selected underground and cut-and-cover waterproofing systems where a protected torch-applied membrane is specified. Final selection should follow the project specification and technical approval process.

### PVC vs HDPE membrane for tunnel waterproofing — which is better?

PVC membranes are flexible sheet systems commonly used with welded joints in tunnel lining applications. HDPE pre-applied membranes are often selected where concrete bonding and high chemical resistance are required. The choice depends on installation method, bonding requirement and project specification.

### How to choose a waterproofing system for underground tunnels?

Start with the tunnel type, waterproofing layer position, groundwater condition, substrate, installation method and required protection layer. Then compare membrane thickness, tensile strength, elongation, puncture resistance, joint treatment and available technical documents.

### What documents are required for tunnel waterproofing membrane procurement?

Request the product technical data sheet, installation guide, selected test reports, packing specification and any project-specific technical recommendations required for engineering approval.
